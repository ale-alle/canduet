Writing music

W
Write: click the staff to write the note length you picked. Clicking a note replaces it. A note too long for the bar goes on, tied, into the next
Shift+click
Add a note to the chord there
V or Esc
Select: click notes or slurs (Shift adds), or drag a box
X
Eraser: click or drag over notes and slurs
T
Tie: drag from a note to the next one of the same pitch; click a tied note to untie it
S
Slur: drag from one note to another. Select it to drag its ends, its middle (height) or the whole slur
Shift+S
Staccato on the selected notes
Ctrl+C, X, V
Copy, cut, paste at the cursor
Backspace
Delete the note here and go back one, so you can delete going backwards
Delete
Delete the selection or the note at the cursor; the cursor stays
↑ ↓ (nothing selected)
Piano: move the cursor to the other staff
R
Record from your MIDI keyboard, after a count-in; R or Space stops
